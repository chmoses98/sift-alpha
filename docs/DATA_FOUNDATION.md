### Blind verification of the other buckets

A stratified blind sample of 95 inputs (verifiers saw only the catalog definition, never the claim): overall agreement **72/95 = 76%**; REQUIRES_COLLECTION: 50/56; EXCLUDED: 15/16; BLOCKED: 7/23. The draft's BLOCKED calls did not survive: see the next paragraph. The sample is evidence about the draft's reliability by bucket; **85% overall agreement is not a licence to start predictive modelling**.

**Two blind rechecks of every drafted-BLOCKED input (143); round 2 under the owner's explicit access policy (no restricted scraping, no paid services, no accounts):** BLOCKED_BY_POLICY 30, BLOCKED_CONFIRMED 19, BLOCKED_CONTESTED 23, RECLASSIFIED_TO_REQUIRES_COLLECTION 71. `BLOCKED_CONFIRMED` = both rounds say blocked; `BLOCKED_BY_POLICY` = round 2 finds the only route is one the policy forbids (round 1 had no policy); `RECLASSIFIED_TO_REQUIRES_COLLECTION` = both rounds find a permitted route; `BLOCKED_CONTESTED` = unresolved disagreement (counted as UNVERIFIED, never as blocked or computable). Round-2 verifiers reported low diligence on several keys (some did not open the catalog), so their answers are evidence, not proof.

**Blind verification of the 132 data-in-hand inputs (no builder existed), two rounds:** DATA_IN_HAND_CONFIRMED 56, DATA_IN_HAND_SCHEMA_ONLY 27, DOWNGRADED_BY_VERIFIER 49. NBA and NHL were re-judged in round 2 on REAL re-read columns (authorized read-only access to the owner's archive repos); a CONFIRMED that rests on a table whose rows were not re-read this session (NBA play-by-play and stints, NHL shot events) is downgraded to schema-only. For the others a downgrade in either round wins and CONFIRMED needs both.

## Per-input detail

Every input has a row in `input_audit.json` and `coverage_matrix.csv`: exact raw-data requirements (need -> satisfying dataset), the catalog provenance sources with canonical ids, status and bucket, the status of the full definition, point-in-time verdict, history coverage (first/last, measured vs documented), freshness, cost, ToS risk, known missingness, blockers, confidence, flags, the earlier computed result and the sibling-repo collection class where one exists, and the reviewer verdict.

## What this assessment does NOT establish

* It does not verify that any catalog definition is correct, that any input predicts anything, or that any collected row is accurate. Missingness is measured only where a ledger or computed table exists; elsewhere it is stated as documented or unmeasured.
* `DATA_IN_HAND` means a profiled archive holds the needed datasets; it does not mean a builder exists or that its leak test has passed. `COLLECT_FREE_UNVERIFIED` rests on documentation, not on a measurement from the runner.
* Sibling-repo archives (nba-edge-finder, NHL-edge-finder, Tennis-Edge-Finder) are credited from their committed ledgers, not re-read here.
* Reviewers were Claude agents working from the registry; their corrections are recorded in `review/<SPORT>.json` with rationales. The registry's access facts were never editable by a reviewer.

## Independent verification protocol (the gate before any predictive experiment)

`verification_sample.json` is a deterministic stratified sample of 82 inputs (sport x bucket) plus 65 dataset status replays; it contains the questions only. The claims live in a separate `verification_answer_key.json` that the verifier must not open before submitting. An independent verifier re-derives each answer and fills `verification_results.json` (template included); `report.score()` compares it with the key. Proposed bar (the owner decides): bucket agreement >= 85% overall and >= 95% on COMPUTABLE; every dataset replay matches; any COMPUTABLE input the verifier rates worse reopens that sport. The verifier must not be the author or a sport reviewer; a human domain expert is preferred. Until a verification is completed and accepted, `research/input_research/phase_control.json` keeps predictive experimentation PAUSED.
