## 3. FULL promotions (owner decision 3)
`full_promotion_audit.json` screens all 177 PARTIAL (or promoted-from-PARTIAL) inputs against the unchanged Phase 8 gate, first failing requirement: no complete blind exact match 92, not PIT_SAFE/pregame-knowable 26, coverage/limits 4, history shorter than the catalog's 21, **clears all of those but declares unimplemented catalog components 33**, already FULL by certification 1. Every input with at most two declared gaps got an individual clause-level reading against the catalog text (`REVIEW` in the module): each gap is a stated catalog clause (a coach- or QB-level effect, a logistic-fit shrink target, a round split, a pitcher-level model, a generalized Pareto fit and so on), not a placeholder step the catalog itself excludes, so none is certified. But the catalog records `pregame_knowable = partial` and the gate requires `yes`. My first reading missed that field; the gate caught it, I reverted the builder claim, the replay and the draft certification record, and left an audit entry. **Genuine FULL promotions this phase: 0.** `ten.home_slam_depth_prior` still fails the 50 % column-coverage gate (majors only), `ten.surface_rating` lacks the catalog's K schedule and Challenger/ITF inputs and has no blind match.
