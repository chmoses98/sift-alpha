## 4. Unresolved issues

### 4.1 Snapshot hash mismatch (blocks the real-data replay)
- The manifest in `required_files.json` was taken from a Tennis-Edge-Finder `tennis-data` snapshot. Its ledger says "2026-11-06", but the access record says `20261109T145505Z`.
- The authorized retrieval from `data/sources/20261109T145505Z/sackmann/` returned all 54 files. **0 of 54 matched**, compared both as gzip bytes and as decompressed CSV.
- Cause (likely, not confirmed): each daily snapshot re-gzips the files, and the gzip header embeds a modification time. So a manifest is valid only for the snapshot it came from.
- **Content identity is undetermined.** The manifest stores one SHA-256 per file, and its source says that is the gzip bytes. So the "decompressed CSV also differs" check in the attempt record compared a CSV hash against a gzip hash, and that can never tell us whether the CSV content changed. We do not know whether the snapshots differ only in gzip headers or also in rows.
  - **Cheapest disambiguation on resumption:** record decompressed-CSV hashes alongside gzip hashes in any new manifest.
- The probable source, `20261106T134626Z`, was outside the authorized path and was never retrieved.
- `load_raw` correctly refuses every mismatched file. The replay was not run, the staged files were deleted, and no raw data is committed.

### 4.2 Missing `best_of` assumptions
- `tennis_ext6` treats a missing `best_of` as best-of-3 everywhere: in the cell keys, the set-count pmf, the completion test (`_sets_ok`) and the calibration cells. The blind implementation made the same choice (`blind/ambiguities.md` items 3, 9 and 13).
- **Risk:** a men's Grand Slam match with a missing `best_of` would be modelled as best-of-3. That corrupts its set distribution and its calibration cell. On the WTA side the assumption is harmless.
- **Not measured:** how often `best_of` is missing in the real Sackmann files.
- **Not fixed:** the same fill is not applied in `tennis_ext2` to `tennis_ext5`. `tennis_ext2._match_outcome` already defaults to 2 sets needed when `best_of` is NaN, but those builders key cells on the raw `best_of`. Missing values therefore form their own NaN group there.
- **Possible fix, not built:** infer `best_of` from `tourney_level` plus tour (ATP `G` → 5; everything else → 3), and flag inferred rows.

### 4.3 Licensing limitations
- Sackmann `tennis_atp` / `tennis_wta` are **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**. The registry entry `sackmann_tennis` says `CLEARED_NONCOMMERCIAL_RESEARCH`, and commercial use is **not cleared**.
- Any model trained on this data, and any derived output, inherits NC-SA. It cannot back a commercial product without a separate licence.
- The upstream repository returned 404 in the 2026-11-13 licensing pass (`docs/PROVIDER_LICENSING_EVIDENCE.md`). The licence evidence dates from Phase 10b and has not been re-confirmed.
- Two further inputs are blocked by missing sources, not licences:
  - `ten.service_points_scenario_mixture` needs a pre-match fitness flag, and no source for it is permitted.
  - `ten.joint_event_correlation` is `BLOCKED_BY_UPSTREAM_INPUT`.
- Kalshi tennis data is subject to the separate Kalshi-terms review in `docs/KALSHI_RIGHTS_IMPACT_AUDIT.md`.

### 4.4 Never measured on real data
Missingness, calibration, real-data PIT leakage, anomalies and coverage were never measured on real data. Known real-data risks:
- WTA serve statistics are missing in about 34% of rows;
- advantage final sets and match tiebreaks are excluded by `_sets_ok`;
- event renames break the `tourney_name` pace-index key.
